Reading and Writing · Module 1 · Question 1 of 27

A tide pool is a small basin of seawater left among the rocks when the ocean draws back. A naturalist who wants to study the animals in the pool cannot work whenever she likes. She must wait until the tide is low, because only then is the pool ____ and easy to reach.

Which choice completes the text with the most logical and precise word or phrase?

The text says the naturalist must wait for low tide, when the pool is uncovered and easy to reach, so the blank needs a word meaning uncovered. "Exposed" fits. "Hidden" describes the pool at high tide, the opposite condition. "Deep" describes the water and would make the pool harder, not easier, to reach. "Distant" points to how far away the pool is, which the sentence never raises.
