Linear Equations in Two Variables

  • Algebra
  • Linear equations in two variables
←PreviousAll Math lessonsNext→

A linear equation in two variables draws a line, and the same line can be written several ways. What the question asks for decides which way you write it.

Key takeaways

  • Every solution (x,y)(x, y)(x,y) is a point on the line.
  • In y=mx+by = mx + by=mx+b, mmm is the slope and bbb is the yyy-intercept.
  • In Ax+By=CAx + By = CAx+By=C, the slope is −AB\frac{-A}{B}B−A​, not AAA, as long as BBB is not zero.
  • Set x=0x = 0x=0 for the yyy-intercept. Set y=0y = 0y=0 for the xxx-intercept.
  • Substitute a coordinate you are given to find the one you are missing.
  • Two distinct nonvertical lines are parallel exactly when their slopes are equal.
  • Perpendicular slopes are negative reciprocals; a vertical line is perpendicular to a horizontal one.

What the SAT may ask

  • Find the slope or an intercept from an equation in any form.
  • Find the missing coordinate of a point on the line.
  • Read a line off a graph or a table and answer a question about it.
  • Write or complete an equation from a slope and a point.
  • Find a coefficient that makes two lines parallel or perpendicular.

The rule

A solution is a pair (x,y)(x, y)(x,y) that makes the equation true, and every solution is a point on the line. So a coordinate you are given can be substituted, and a coordinate you are missing can be solved for.

Slope-intercept form is y=mx+by = mx + by=mx+b. It shows the slope mmm and the yyy-intercept bbb.

Standard form is Ax+By=CAx + By = CAx+By=C, with AAA and BBB not both zero. The slope and the intercepts are all in there; they are just less direct to read.

To move to the other form, subtract the xxx term from both sides, then divide every term by BBB. That needs a BBB that is not zero. When BBB is zero and AAA is not, the equation is a vertical line, which has no slope to find.

4x+3y=184x + 3y = 184x+3y=18 3y=−4x+183y = -4x + 183y=−4x+18 y=−43x+6y = \frac{-4}{3}x + 6y=3−4​x+6

Slope: −43\frac{-4}{3}3−4​. yyy-intercept: 666.

Intercepts need no conversion. A point on the yyy-axis has an xxx-coordinate of zero, and a point on the xxx-axis has a yyy-coordinate of zero. Substitute the zero and solve, on any form.

Two points give the slope, as long as their xxx-coordinates differ. A slope and one point give the line, in point-slope form, with no separate step to find bbb.

m=y2−y1x2−x1m = \frac{y_2 - y_1}{x_2 - x_1}m=x2​−x1​y2​−y1​​ y−y1=m(x−x1)y - y_1 = m(x - x_1)y−y1​=m(x−x1​)

Parallel lines never meet. Two distinct nonvertical lines are parallel exactly when their slopes are equal, and two distinct vertical lines are parallel with no slope between them.

Perpendicular lines meet at a right angle. When both slopes exist and neither is zero, they are negative reciprocals, mmm and −1m\frac{-1}{m}m−1​. A vertical line is perpendicular to a horizontal one, and no reciprocal shows it.

Why the rule exists

The slope is a rate. It says how much yyy moves for each 111 that xxx moves. In y=mx+by = mx + by=mx+b that rate sits alone in front of the xxx, where you can read it.

In Ax+By=CAx + By = CAx+By=C the same rate is split across two coefficients. It is not AAA, not BBB, and not AB\frac{A}{B}BA​. It is −AB\frac{-A}{B}B−A​ whenever BBB is not zero. The minus is there because you subtract the xxx term from both sides before you divide.

The arithmetic is one subtraction and one division. Decide which number you divide by before you start: the coefficient of yyy, never the coefficient of xxx.

Worked examples

1. Read a model in standard form

A community kitchen has 909090 minutes of oven time. A batch of loaves needs 303030 minutes and a batch of rolls needs 151515 minutes, so 30x+15y=9030x + 15y = 9030x+15y=90. Here xxx is the number of loaf batches and yyy is the number of roll batches. What is the yyy-intercept of this equation's graph, and what does it mean here?

Question asks: the yyy-intercept, and what it stands for.

First move: substitute x=0x = 0x=0, which in this story means baking no loaves at all.

30(0)+15y=9030(0) + 15y = 9030(0)+15y=90

Divide both sides by 151515.

y=6y = 6y=6

Answer: 666 batches of rolls, which is the most that fit if no loaves are baked.

Each coefficient is minutes per batch, and the 909090 is the total time available. Every number in a model means something in its own story, and naming what each one means is most of what an interpretation question asks for.

The line holds more points than the story does. A batch count cannot be negative or fractional, so only whole nonnegative (x,y)(x, y)(x,y) on this line are plans the kitchen could bake.

2. Use the shared y-intercept

In the xyxyxy-plane, line ppp is the graph of y=3x−7y = 3x - 7y=3x−7. Line qqq has the same yyy-intercept as line ppp and passes through (2,5)(2, 5)(2,5). What is the slope of line qqq?

Question asks: the slope of line qqq.

First move: read line ppp's yyy-intercept, which line qqq shares.

Line ppp is already solved for yyy, so its yyy-intercept is −7-7−7. Line qqq has the same one, so line qqq is y=mx−7y = mx - 7y=mx−7 with mmm still unknown.

The point (2,5)(2, 5)(2,5) is on line qqq, so substitute both of its coordinates.

5=2m−75 = 2m - 75=2m−7

Add 777 to both sides.

12=2m12 = 2m12=2m

Divide both sides by 222.

m=6m = 6m=6

Answer: 666

Check: you built this equation yourself, so put the point back into it. 6(2)−76(2) - 76(2)−7 gives 555.

The 333 is line ppp's slope and has nothing to do with line qqq. Nothing here needed converting, because both lines were already written in the form that shows what was wanted.

3. Match the slopes to find a coefficient

In the xyxyxy-plane, the graph of 6x+ky=56x + ky = 56x+ky=5 is parallel to the graph of y=2x+1y = 2x + 1y=2x+1, where kkk is a constant. What is the value of kkk?

Question asks: the constant kkk.

First move: write both slopes, then set them equal.

The second line is already solved for yyy, so its slope is 222. Solve the first for yyy: subtract 6x6x6x, then divide every term by kkk.

y=−6kx+5ky = \frac{-6}{k}x + \frac{5}{k}y=k−6​x+k5​

Its slope is −6k\frac{-6}{k}k−6​, and parallel means the two slopes are equal.

−6k=2\frac{-6}{k} = 2k−6​=2

Multiply both sides by kkk.

−6=2k-6 = 2k−6=2k

Divide both sides by 222.

k=−3k = -3k=−3

Answer: −3-3−3

Common mistake: 333 comes from dropping the minus, which appears when you subtract 6x6x6x from both sides. A positive kkk would make this line fall, and the line it has to match rises.

Special cases

Vertical and horizontal lines

A vertical line is x=ax = ax=a. Every point on it has the same xxx-coordinate. Its slope is undefined rather than zero, because the run between any two of its points is zero and division by zero is undefined. It has no yyy-intercept unless it is the yyy-axis itself.

A horizontal line is y=cy = cy=c. Its slope is 000, and it has no xxx-intercept unless it is the xxx-axis.

A vertical line and a horizontal line are perpendicular, and the negative reciprocal will not show it: −1m\frac{-1}{m}m−1​ needs a slope, and a vertical line has none. Recognize this pair rather than computing it.

Common traps

Do not confuse a coefficient with the slope

The forms hide different things, and an equation that has not been converted still has numbers in it. Asked for the slope of 10x+5y=4010x + 5y = 4010x+5y=40, the eye lands on the 101010. The slope is −2-2−2. Asked for the yyy-intercept of the same line, the eye lands on the 404040. The intercept is 888.

Trap Quick check
Reading AAA as the slope in Ax+By=CAx + By = CAx+By=C The slope is −AB\frac{-A}{B}B−A​, if BBB is not zero
Dividing only some terms by BBB Divide every term, the xxx term included
Answering the slope when the ask was a coefficient Re-read what you wrote in step 1

A choice that matches a number printed in the question is worth a second look. It is not automatically wrong. The yyy-intercept of y=9x−4y = 9x - 4y=9x−4 really is −4-4−4. Ask whether the equation in front of you is the one that displays what you were asked for.

The method: Ask, form, solve, answer

  1. Name the ask. A slope, an intercept, a coordinate, or a number inside the equation. Write it down before you start.
  2. Choose the form that shows it. A slope: solve for yyy. An intercept: substitute the zero. A missing coordinate: substitute the one you were given. A parallel or perpendicular line: start from the given line's direction, which is its slope, or no slope at all if it is vertical. An unknown coefficient: work in the equation you were handed.
  3. Do the algebra. Undo in reverse order, one operation at a time. When you solve for yyy, divide every term by the coefficient of yyy, its sign included. A special case needs none of this: a vertical or horizontal line is recognized, not computed.
  4. Answer the ask. Give the quantity you named in step 1. Substitute back when you built an equation on the way, or when you read a number off a figure. A wrong equation and a misread graph still produce a number.

When the built-in calculator helps

A graphing calculator is built into the testing app and is allowed on every Math question.

Graph an equation and the form you rewrote it into when you want to check the rewrite. The same line means one graph, not two.

Graph an equation and read its intercepts off the axes when the numbers are awkward enough that you would not trust the arithmetic.

Finding a slope or an intercept from a clean equation is faster by hand.

Practice

Work each item with the method, under a rough clock: about a minute for a bare equation, and up to about two minutes once there is a context to translate or a graph or table to read first. The Math section gives you about 95 seconds a question on average, so that range is test-day pace rather than a drill speed. Press Show solution to see the steps, one to a line.

Correct. Substituting the given xxx-coordinate and dividing by the coefficient of yyy gives 444.

The method, applied

Step 1. The ask is kkk, the yyy-coordinate of a point on the line.

Step 2. A point on the line makes the equation true, so substitute it: 333 for xxx, and kkk for yyy.

2(3)+3k=182(3) + 3k = 182(3)+3k=18

Step 3. Multiply out the left side.

6+3k=186 + 3k = 186+3k=18

Subtract 666 from both sides.

3k=123k = 123k=12

Divide both sides by 333.

k=4k = 4k=4

Step 4. The ask was kkk, so the answer is 444.

  • Standard

The graph of Ax−2y=6Ax - 2y = 6Ax−2y=6 in the xyxyxy-plane is shown, where AAA is a constant. What is the value of AAA?

Correct. Substituting the graph's xxx-intercept (2,0)(2, 0)(2,0) and dividing by 222 gives 333.

The method, applied

Step 1. The ask is AAA, a coefficient. It is not the slope, and here the two are not equal.

Step 2. Every point on the line makes the equation true, and the graph marks two of them. Take the xxx-intercept (2,0)(2, 0)(2,0): a yyy-coordinate of zero removes the yyy term, which leaves one unknown.

Step 3. Substitute both coordinates.

A(2)−2(0)=6A(2) - 2(0) = 6A(2)−2(0)=6

Simplify the left side.

2A=62A = 62A=6

Divide both sides by 222.

A=3A = 3A=3

Step 4. You read this point off the figure, so check the line the value produces against the whole graph. Solve 3x−2y=63x - 2y = 63x−2y=6 for yyy.

y=32x−3y = \frac{3}{2}x - 3y=23​x−3

Its slope and both its intercepts are the ones drawn.

Substituting the yyy-intercept (0,−3)(0, -3)(0,−3) would have checked nothing: an xxx-coordinate of zero removes the AAA term, so that point fits every value of AAA. The ask was AAA, so the answer is 333.

Step 1. The ask is kkk, a coefficient. It is not a slope, and the two are not equal here.

−k6=13\frac{-k}{6} = \frac{1}{3}6−k​=31​

−k=2-k = 2−k=2

k=−2k = -2k=−2

Step 4. The ask was kkk, so the answer is −2-2−2.

In the xyxyxy-plane, line ℓ\ellℓ passes through (−4,1)(-4, 1)(−4,1) and (−4,7)(-4, 7)(−4,7). Line kkk is perpendicular to line ℓ\ellℓ and passes through (3,2)(3, 2)(3,2). At what value of yyy does line kkk cross the yyy-axis?

Correct. Line ℓ\ellℓ is vertical, so line kkk is horizontal at the height of the point it passes through, which is 222.

y=2y = 2y=2

Step 4. That holds at every point on line kkk, including the one on the yyy-axis. The ask was that yyy-coordinate, so the answer is 222.
