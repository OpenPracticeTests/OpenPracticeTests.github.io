How to use

How to use this site

All tests and study guides are free. No account is needed.

Pick a test

Choose a test from the home page or the Tests menu. Everything here is for the SAT. Other exams are planned and nothing is built for them yet.

Take a practice test

Select Start on the practice tests page. Tests follow the exam's module order and time limits, with a separate timer for each module.

Answer one question at a time. Flag questions to revisit, or use the question grid to move between them. The tools above each question let you cross out choices, highlight passage text, and open a formula sheet in math sections.

Your work saves automatically, so closing the tab does not erase it. The timer keeps running while you use tools or switch tabs. Pause the test when you take a break.

Submitting a module locks it, and you cannot return to it during the test. Results appear after you submit the final module.

Browse without a timer

Select Browse on the practice tests page to work without a timer. Open any module or question, then press Show solution below the choices to see the correct answer and the explanation. Reset this question hides them again and clears your answer. There is nothing to submit.

See your score

Results show how many questions you got right, wrong, and left blank, out of every question on the test. You get the same three counts by section and by category, and a chart of the time spent on each question against whether you answered it correctly.

There is no predicted exam score here. We have no way to check one against the real scale, and the real exam is adaptive, so a fixed test cannot reproduce its scoring. Our three levels are also not the same difficulty, so the same percentage on two different tests does not mean the same thing.

Review your answers

Review shows your chosen answer, the correct answer, a worked explanation, the topic, and the time spent on each question. Answers cannot be changed in review.

Read the study guides

Study guides are organized by tested topic. Each lesson explains a rule, shows worked examples and common mistakes, and includes a step-by-step method and practice questions.

Many topics also have a practice bank with extra questions, shown one at a time with answers hidden until you reveal them. Use the Previous and Next links on each lesson to follow the guide in order.

Print

Press Ctrl+P, or Cmd+P on a Mac, on a test page to print the full test without answers.

After finishing a test, select Generate PDF report to print or save your attempt, including every question, your answer, the correct answer, and the explanation. You can also print study guides and topic pages.

Download interactive report saves the same thing as one file you can open in any browser. It needs no internet, and the time charts still work: point at a bar or a table row to see the question, or select one to jump to it.

Your progress

Test progress stays in the browser and device you use. It is not sent to a server or shared across browsers or devices. Clearing your browsing data deletes it.

Lesson drills and practice banks start fresh each time you open the page. Save a PDF report to keep a copy of your results.
